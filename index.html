SplitPool Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

SplitPool ("the app") is published by FatPotato. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

  • Name you enter as a display name and member names in a pool.
  • Pool and expense details you enter: amounts collected, expenses, notes and activity log entries.
  • Anonymous account ID created by Firebase Authentication so you can join pools. We do not ask for your email, phone or password.

How we use it

Only to provide the app's features: syncing pools between members, showing balances and the activity log. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, and the app currently shows no ads.

Storage and security

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and encrypted in transit. Only members of a pool can read that pool's data.

Deleting your data

The pool collector can delete a pool, which removes its data. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page.
